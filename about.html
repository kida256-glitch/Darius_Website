Our Story

Born from Reverence

Where tradition meets divine artistry

Likeness restored.
Identity honored.

Est. March 2026 · Kampala

Eikon Graphē started in March 2026, founded by Abaho Darious alongside a team of artists united by like passion.

Rooted in the Greek words Eikon (image, likeness) and Graphē (to draw, to inscribe), the name reflects more than portraiture — it embodies a calling. Each line is not merely sketched, but carefully inscribed as an act of honor. Each shadow is placed with intention. Each highlight whispers of light.

At Eikon Graphē, portraiture is a royal craft — inspired by tradition, guided by spiritual depth. We believe every face carries history, dignity, and divine design. Through graphite and silence, we seek to reveal not only appearance, but essence.

This is not simply art. It is likeness restored. It is identity honored. It is truth drawn in light and defined in shadow.

Our work is characterized by meticulous attention to detail, reverence for the subject, and a commitment to creating portraits that transcend time. We approach each commission as a sacred trust, understanding that we are capturing not just a face, but a legacy.

Every stroke of graphite is deliberate. Every gradation of tone is purposeful. We work in the quiet spaces where observation meets interpretation, where technical skill serves deeper vision.

AD

Abaho Darious

Founder & Lead Artist

With a vision to elevate pencil portraiture to its rightful place among the fine arts, Darious established Eikon Graphē as a studio where craftsmanship and reverence converge. His approach combines classical technique with contemporary sensibility, creating portraits that honor both tradition and innovation.
