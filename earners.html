Become an Earner

Join our network of ambassadors and earn through referrals

Turn your network into income by connecting people with timeless portrait art.

The Eikon Graphē Earners Program invites you to become a valued partner in our mission to preserve legacies through portraiture. As an Earner, you'll receive generous commissions for every successful referral, while helping families and individuals honor their loved ones.

Whether you're a creative professional, event planner, photographer, or simply someone with a passion for fine art, this program offers a meaningful way to generate income while promoting work that matters.

  • Earn competitive commissions on every completed portrait
  • Access to exclusive marketing materials and resources
  • Priority support and dedicated account management
  • Flexible partnership with no quotas or obligations
  • Monthly payouts via mobile money or bank transfer
  • Recognition in our partner network

Getting started is simple. Fill out our partnership form, and we'll provide you with everything you need to begin referring clients. Track your referrals, monitor your earnings, and receive payments seamlessly.
