Our Story

Born from Reverence

Eikon Graphē started in March 2026, founded by Abaho Darious alongside a team of artists united by like passion.

Rooted in the Greek words Eikon (image, likeness) and Graphē (to draw, to inscribe), the name reflects more than portraiture — it embodies a calling. Each line is not merely sketched, but carefully inscribed as an act of honor. Each shadow is placed with intention. Each highlight whispers of light.

At Eikon Graphē, portraiture is a royal craft — inspired by tradition, guided by spiritual depth. We believe every face carries history, dignity, and divine design. Through graphite and silence, we seek to reveal not only appearance, but essence.

This is not simply art. It is likeness restored. It is identity honored. It is truth drawn in light and defined in shadow.

AD
Abaho Darious Founder & Lead Artist

Commission

For Orders

We are glad of your willingness to commission us as we help your memories captured on paper and your identity honored.

01

Upload Reference Photo

We accept selfies and real-time soft copy pictures. Clarity of the face is key to capturing your likeness faithfully.

02

State Your Specifications

Clearly state the size of your final masterpiece (A2 or A3), your full name, and your email or phone number.

03

Confirm Payment Plan

Contact us to confirm your payment plan. We offer flexible arrangements to suit every client.

04

Give Us Your Deadline

Share your deadline for the order. Your order is immediately started to ensure we meet your timeline.

05

Work Begins

Our artist begins immediately upon order confirmation. Every stroke is made with intention and care for your legacy.

06

Delivery

A well-framed Eikon Graphē portrait, packaged protectively, is delivered right to you. Timeless. Intentional. Yours.
