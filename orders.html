Commission Process

How to Order

A simple, transparent process from consultation to delivery

Every portrait begins with a conversation. We take time to understand your vision, discuss reference materials, and ensure the final piece honors your expectations.

01

Initial Contact

Reach out via WhatsApp to discuss your portrait needs. Share your vision, reference photos, and any specific requirements.

02

Consultation

We'll review your references, discuss sizing, style preferences, and provide a detailed quote with timeline estimates.

03

Deposit & Start

Upon agreement, a 50% deposit secures your commission slot. We begin work and keep you updated throughout the process.

04

Progress Updates

Receive regular progress photos and have opportunities for feedback during key stages of the portrait development.

05

Final Review

View the completed portrait and request any minor adjustments. Your satisfaction is our priority before final delivery.

06

Delivery

After final payment, your portrait is carefully packaged and delivered. Each piece includes a certificate of authenticity.
